01 · Game identity, pillars and boundaries · 20 entries

The guiding promise

This is an original, grounded 3D city-and-cultivation life simulator. The player begins with a modest growing space and gradually earns a place in a town that has its own routines. Gameplay, character development and a believable world take priority over token marketing. The reference is the slower, more tactile feeling of early GameCube-era farm life—not direct copies of anyone else’s characters, maps, or assets.

Build checkpoint: A complete and enjoyable daily-life loop must work before expansion or monetization is considered.
