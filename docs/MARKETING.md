# POTIFY City Public World Bible marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** World-builders, game fans and illustrators. **Offer.** Let players and contributors explore the city while keeping story discoveries intact.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show one city concept beside its current implementation status and an invitation to follow development. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Choose a district and submit one annotated idea. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 specific worldbuilding responses. |

### Sales test

Print a one-district proof. Target three price-confirmed expressions of interest before taking preorders, then report printing and shipping costs against paid copies. Test demand for a printable city field guide through opt-ins, then paid preorders only with deliverable art and terms.

### Measurement and decision

Use `city-canon-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The 469-entry public registry is a design record. The game runtime lives in the separate Potify repository. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
